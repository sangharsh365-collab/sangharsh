Learning Path

Beginner

Rules, basic mates, opening principles, tactics patterns (forks, pins), and simple pawn endings.

Daily (15–20′): 5 tactics • 10 opening moves • 1 endgame drill

Intermediate

Structure play (IQP, Carlsbad), calculation, typical piece sacrifices, rook endings (Lucena/Philidor).

Daily (30′): Theme tactics • 10′ endgame • 1 model game

Advanced

Opening prep trees, prophylaxis, technical conversion, practical decision-making.

Daily (45′): Deep calc set • complex endgame • novelties review

Tactics Themes & Training

Core Motifs

  • Forks, pins, skewers
  • Discovered attack/check
  • Deflection & decoy
  • Clearance & interference
  • Attraction & zwischenzug

Advanced Patterns

  • Zugzwang & undermining
  • Greek Gift patterns
  • Underpromotion ideas
  • Desperado resources
  • Counter-check motifs

Daily Plan (10–20′)

Pick 1–2 motifs, solve 10 puzzles by theme, then review a short master miniature using the same pattern.

Endgame Essentials

Must-know basics

  • King activity & opposition
  • K+Q vs K, K+R vs K techniques
  • K+P vs K (key squares, triangulation)
  • Outside passer & passed pawn races

Rook & Minor-piece endings

  • Lucena & Philidor positions
  • Cut-off king, active rooks
  • Good knight vs bad bishop
  • Opposite-colored bishop dynamics

Grandmasters & Study Focus

Garry Kasparov

Initiative-driven, dynamic attacks. Study exchange sacs and active piece play.

Bobby Fischer

Opening prep + endgame precision. Model games in 1.e4 and queen endings.

Magnus Carlsen

Universal style, technical squeezes. Study small-edge conversions.

Viswanathan Anand

Lightning calculation & sharp prep. Review rapid-play masterpieces.

José R. Capablanca

Positional purity; instructive endgames. Simplify to favorable endings.

Alexander Alekhine

Combinational attacks from quiet positions. Study coordination & themes.
